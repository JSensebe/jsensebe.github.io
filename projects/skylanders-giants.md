# **Skylanders: Giants**

### **Platform(s):** Nintendo 3DS  
### **Role:** Gameplay Engineer  
### **Engine / Tech:** Toys for Bob's proprietary game engine  
### **Year:** 2012  

---

## **Overview**
*Skylanders: Giants* is an action‑adventure title developed by nSpace for the Nintendo 3DS. My work focused on implementing gameplay features and supporting level designers under a tight production schedule. While the project didn’t involve deep engine modification or hardware‑level tricks, it required rapid iteration, clear communication, and reliable gameplay engineering to meet deadlines.

---

## **My Responsibilities**
- Implemented gameplay logic for enemies, hazards, and interactive objects  
- Integrated designer‑driven gameplay scripts into the engine  
- Tuned combat interactions and enemy behaviors  
- Implemented small gameplay features and polish tasks across multiple levels  
- Fixed gameplay bugs and stability issues discovered late in development  
- Supported level designers by implementing requested interactions and behaviors  
- Ensured gameplay systems behaved consistently across levels  

---

## **Technical Highlights**

### **1. Rapid Gameplay Feature Implementation**
Because of the compressed schedule, many gameplay features needed to be implemented quickly and cleanly. I delivered small but essential gameplay systems — enemy triggers, hazards, pickups, scripted events — with minimal iteration time.

### **2. Designer‑Driven Scripting Integration**
I worked closely with level designers to translate their gameplay intentions into functional in‑engine behaviors. This included implementing scripted sequences, enemy spawns, and environmental interactions.

### **3. Combat and Interaction Polish**
I contributed to tuning hit reactions, timing, and readability for enemies and hazards. These adjustments helped maintain clarity and responsiveness in moment‑to‑moment gameplay.

---

## **Engineering Challenges**

### **Working Under a Tight Production Schedule**
The biggest challenge on *Skylanders: Giants* wasn’t technical complexity — it was time. Many gameplay tasks needed to be implemented quickly to keep level production moving. This required writing clean, reliable code with minimal iteration.

### **Supporting Multiple Designers Simultaneously**
With several levels in active development, I often handled requests from multiple designers at once. Clear communication and predictable implementation were essential to avoid blocking level progress.

### **Maintaining Stability Late in Development**
Late‑stage bug fixing required careful attention to avoid regressions. I focused on resolving gameplay issues without introducing new problems, especially in levels that were nearly content‑complete.

---

## **Collaboration**
I worked closely with level designers, animators, and QA throughout development. Designers relied on me to implement gameplay interactions quickly and reliably. QA provided rapid feedback on gameplay bugs, which I addressed to maintain stability as the project approached deadlines.

---

## **Outcome**
*Skylanders: Giants* shipped on Nintendo 3DS in 2012. My contributions helped keep level production on schedule and ensured gameplay systems behaved consistently across the game. While not the most technically demanding project in my portfolio, it strengthened my ability to deliver reliable gameplay engineering under tight deadlines.
